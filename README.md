# Gobble Dash — A Turkey's Great Escape

Thanksgiving is coming, and Gobble the turkey is not on the menu. Run 1500 metres
from the farmyard, through the barnyard, and out of the kitchen — past ovens,
carving knives, rolling pins and gravy — while the farmer closes in behind you.

**What the run has:** 3 zones with their own speed, hazards and palette
(Farmyard → Barnyard → Kitchen) · a farmer who chases, searches and corners
you — he gains on every stumble and falls back on every 5-corn streak ·
5 hazards, each with its own answer (oven: change lanes, knife: time it,
rolling pin and hay bale: jump, gravy: jump or dash) · corn combos up to x5 ·
golden feather shield and pumpkin pie +1 life · haystack hiding (press ↓
inside a haystack) · dash with a cooldown · synthesized WebAudio sound
(gobble, chimes, thud, fanfare) and a bass loop — no audio files.

**Controls:** ↑ / ↓ switch lanes · Space jump · Shift dash · ↓ in a haystack
to hide · P pause · M mute · mouse: click a lane to run there, click the
turkey to jump. Enter starts and restarts.

**Play it:** serve `game/` with any static server (`node serve.ts`, then
open http://localhost:8080) — it plays offline in desktop Chrome, no network
request. Screenshots in `screenshots/` are real renders of this code
(`?shot=title|play|kitchen|escaped|caught&seed=1`).
